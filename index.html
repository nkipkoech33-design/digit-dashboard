🧠 ADAPTIVE LEARNING

Signal history tracking: every signal that fires (grade A or B) is recorded and its outcome is resolved on the next tick. Each market builds its own win/loss record of fired signals.

Auto-pause: if a market's last 10+ signals won less than 40% of the time, signals from that market are blocked until its accuracy recovers.

Position sizing: grade A → 1.5–2% of capital · grade B → 0.75–1% · grade C → skip.

🎯 LIVE FLOW GATE

Every TRADE verdict now requires live tick-flow confirmation. The bot reads the last 40 ticks and computes EVEN% vs ODD% (or OVER% vs UNDER%, or RISE% vs FALL%).

Blocked → WAIT if the opposing side is dominant (≥ 55%)
Confirmed → TRADE if any of these holds: target side ≥ 55% · all hot digits on target side · target strengthening ≥ +5pp · opposing side weakening ≤ −5pp

Result: TRADE EVEN will never fire while ODD is dominant, and vice-versa.
